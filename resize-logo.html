Quick Download Steps:

  • Right-click on the 32x32 logo above
  • Select "Save image as..."
  • Save as favicon.ico in the public/ folder
  • Refresh your browser (Ctrl+F5) to see the new favicon

Why the favicon might not show:

  • Browser cache - try Ctrl+F5 to hard refresh
  • Some browsers prefer .ico files over PNG
  • Favicon might take a few minutes to update
  • Try opening in an incognito/private window
