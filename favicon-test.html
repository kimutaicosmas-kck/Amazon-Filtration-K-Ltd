If favicon still shows white:

  • Hard refresh: Press Ctrl+Shift+R or Ctrl+F5
  • Clear cache: Go to browser settings and clear cache
  • Incognito mode: Open in private/incognito window
  • Different browser: Try Chrome, Firefox, or Edge
  • Wait a few minutes: Sometimes favicons take time to update

Alternative Solution:

  • Right-click on the 32x32 logo above
  • Save as "favicon.ico" in the public folder
  • This creates a proper ICO file from your logo
